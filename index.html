About

View academic CV ↗

I am an M.Sc. student in Chemical Modelling at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore, supervised by Assoc. Prof. Ran Ni. My current research focuses on Monte Carlo modelling of depletion-driven colloidal assembly, including how deviations in particle shape influence chiral selection and self-sorting.

Previously, I worked as an undergraduate research assistant at the Center for Materials Surface Science, UESTC, under Professors Dehui Wang and Xu Deng. There, I investigated durable superhydrophobic coatings and droplet–surface interactions. I hold B.Eng. degrees from UESTC and the University of Glasgow.

Research interests

  • Wetting & interfacial transport
  • Functional surface coatings
  • Particle geometry & self-assembly
  • Monte Carlo simulation

COMPUTATIONAL SOFT MATTER

From particle shape to colloidal assembly

Investigating how particle geometry and depletion interactions influence collective ordering, chiral selection and self-sorting.

C++NVT / NPTImplicit depletion
Current research · NTU
Research details

I am developing a modular C++ Monte Carlo framework for hard particles and implicit ideal depletants. The work combines accelerated overlap detection, checkpoint/restart and literature benchmarks, and compares pairwise and many-body descriptions of depletion.

CONCEPTUAL ILLUSTRATION

SURFACE & INTERFACE ENGINEERING

Durable surfaces for water shedding

Designing sprayable, waterborne superhydrophobic coatings that combine hierarchical texture with practical durability.

Surface chemistryHierarchical coatingsWet radomes
Research at UESTC · 2023–2026
Research details

My work at UESTC included formulation screening, silica surface functionalisation, spray deposition, dynamic wetting measurements, abrasion and environmental ageing tests. One project uses silica nanoparticles and hollow glass microspheres to combine water shedding with a low-loss coating architecture for radomes.

CONCEPTUAL ILLUSTRATION

DROPLET–SURFACE INTERACTIONS

Watching droplets meet structured materials

Using high-speed imaging and wetting measurements to understand droplet impact, penetration and liquid transport.

High-speed imaging10,000 fpsPorous meshes
Experimental research · UESTC
Research details

I assisted droplet-impact testing and 10,000-fps video production for a superhydrophilic–Janus bilayer window screen, and co-analysed penetration data. This work was published in Small in 2025. Read the paper ↗
